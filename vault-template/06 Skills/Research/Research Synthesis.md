# Research Synthesis

## Purpose
Combine multiple verified sources into a concise view without flattening disagreements or uncertainty.

## Trigger
Several sources answer the same research question or a weekly/monthly synthesis is requested.

## Inputs
Verified evidence set, claims, conflicts, dates, authority, scope.

## Procedure
Group evidence by sub-question; identify consensus, disagreement, chronology, and applicability; preserve minority/conflicting evidence; state inference separately; produce conclusions only as strongly as evidence supports; link durable reusable ideas into concepts/frameworks.

## Verification
Every material conclusion maps to evidence and any conflict remains visible.

## Failure Conditions
Evidence set is too weak/unrelated or synthesis would require unsupported assumptions.

## Outputs
Research synthesis plus candidate concepts/claims/questions.

## Side Effects
May create provisional derived knowledge through compiler/transaction policy.

## Permission Level
Level 1.

## Example
Three sources support one mechanism while a fourth contradicts it in a different context; the synthesis reports both and the context boundary.
