# Deep Research

## Purpose
Answer a research question by collecting, separating, and synthesizing evidence while keeping external research distinct from existing brain knowledge.

## Trigger
User asks for deep research or a knowledge gap is intentionally researched.

## Inputs
Research question, scope, existing brain evidence, allowed external tools/sources, egress policy.

## Procedure
Clarify scope from the question; retrieve existing brain knowledge first; identify missing evidence; gather allowed external evidence; preserve/import useful sources when appropriate; record findings, disagreements, dates, authority, and unknowns; synthesize only after evidence collection; route durable conclusions through normal compilation.

## Verification
Claims are traceable to sources; source-derived statements are distinguished from inference; conflicts and missing evidence are visible.

## Failure Conditions
Insufficient evidence, inaccessible sources, egress restriction, or inability to verify a material claim.

## Outputs
Research note/source set, candidate compiled knowledge, unresolved questions.

## Side Effects
May ingest external source material and create provisional knowledge through normal policy.

## Permission Level
Level 0/1; external publication/action is not included.

## Example
Researching a technology choice first retrieves existing project decisions, then adds current primary-source evidence and produces a source-backed comparison.
